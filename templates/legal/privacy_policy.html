Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Information We Collect

EchoDesk ("we," "our," or "us") collects the following information when you use our Facebook integration:

  • Facebook Page Information: Page name, Page ID, and basic page metadata
  • Messages: Messages sent to your Facebook pages by customers
  • User Account Information: Your name, email, and basic profile information needed to manage your account

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Display customer messages from your Facebook pages in the EchoDesk dashboard
  • Provide customer service management and ticketing functionality
  • Send notifications about new messages and system updates
  • Improve our service and user experience

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties except:

  • When required by law
  • To protect our rights, property, or safety
  • With your explicit consent

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. This includes:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Limited access to personal information on a need-to-know basis

5. Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law. You can request deletion of your data at any time.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Export your data

7. Facebook, Messenger and Instagram

Businesses can connect their Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts to EchoDesk to answer customer messages from a shared customer-support inbox. When they do:

Data we collect

  • Page and account data: Page / Instagram account ID, name, username and profile picture of the connected accounts.
  • Access tokens: the Page access token Meta issues when an administrator connects a Page, plus the app-scoped Facebook user ID of the administrator who granted it. Tokens are stored server-side only and are used solely to receive and send the Page's messages.
  • Messages: messages, attachments and reactions exchanged between customers and the connected Page or Instagram account (we never access anyone's personal inbox).
  • Sender profile: the customer's page-scoped ID, display name and profile picture as provided by Meta, so agents can see who they are talking to.

How we use it

  • Only to operate the business's customer-support inbox: show conversations to the business's agents, deliver their replies, route and assign chats, and show conversation history.
  • Replies sent more than 24 hours after a customer's last message use Meta's Human Agent tag. They are always written and sent by a human agent, never automatically, and only within 7 days of the customer's last message.
  • AI processing (suggested or automatic replies, conversation summaries) happens only when the business explicitly enables EchoDesk's AI features. Message text is then sent to our AI provider solely to generate that output. EchoDesk does not use this data to train AI models. Automated replies are only sent inside Meta's standard 24-hour window.
  • We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties other than the service providers needed to run EchoDesk (hosting, storage, and the optional AI provider).

Retention

  • Messages are kept for as long as the business keeps the channel connected and its EchoDesk account active, or until the business deletes them.
  • When a Page or Instagram account is disconnected in EchoDesk settings, or the EchoDesk app is removed from Facebook, that connection is deactivated and its stored access token is deleted immediately. Message history is kept for the business until it deletes it or closes its account.
  • A business can permanently delete a channel's message history from EchoDesk settings at any time, or ask us to close its EchoDesk account by emailing support@echodesk.ge; closing the account deletes its workspace, including its conversation data.

Revoking access

You can disconnect a Page or Instagram account at any time in EchoDesk settings, or remove the EchoDesk app in your Facebook settings (Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations). We process Meta's deauthorization notice automatically. We comply with the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies.

8. Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data:

  • Through Facebook: remove the EchoDesk app in your Facebook settings and choose to send a deletion request. Meta notifies us through our data deletion callback; we delete the Page connections and access tokens tied to your Facebook account and give you a confirmation code and a status page where you can follow the request.
  • By email: write to support@echodesk.ge. If you messaged a business that uses EchoDesk and want those conversations removed, include the Page or Instagram account you wrote to; we will delete them together with that business.
  • See the data deletion instructions for details.

We complete deletion requests within 30 days.
